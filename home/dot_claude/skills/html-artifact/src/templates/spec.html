Overview

Participants need to download their beneficiary designations as a PDF or CSV for their records. Today they must call support to get a copy. This spec covers the self-serve export flow accessible from the Beneficiaries tab.

Decision — CSV only in v1. PDF generation adds a server-side rendering dependency that isn't worth the complexity for the initial release. PDF can follow as a separate ticket.

User Flow

  1. Participant opens the Beneficiaries tab.
  2. Clicks Export in the top-right action bar.
  3. A modal confirms the scope: all active beneficiaries, current designations only.
  4. CSV downloads immediately — no email, no async job.
Decision — Synchronous download, not async + email. The dataset is small (≤20 rows per participant) and instant is better UX than "check your email".

The confirmation modal also lets participants cancel. No destructive action occurs — it exists only to set expectations about what the export contains, preventing support calls from participants surprised by the format.
